Johnson Do

"Art is to Comfort The Disturbed, and to Disturb the Comfortable" - Cesar A. Cruz Seattle native, working in the game industry since 2018. My name is Johnson Do, 33, Seattle-based graphic designer and illustrator with a background in working with mobile game companies, and businesses to create their high level creative needs. I will always go beyond to make my clients happy and to make sure that I always exceed their expectations. Profficient in: Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Blender 4.0+, Unity 2D/3D I have studied fine arts at Cornish College of the Arts for 2 years. Besides the fact that I didn’t complete the 4 years program, I utilized my skills that were self-taught that got me into the school after I left to create my own business where I sell 100% original products. Through the skills that I’ve obtained through running my own business from 2016, I have learned how to market myself through social media and printed marketing goods, how to book events/pop-up shows/ night markets/ fairs/ conventions, how to manage a business, create shipping solutions, package products, get into a retail shop, etc. I have worked with a few clients already and would love to provide my expertise for your projects. Past clients include Amazon, Scalisco, Indie Game Studios.
